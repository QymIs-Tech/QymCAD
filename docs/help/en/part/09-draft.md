# Draft

Select the body, click the faces to be tilted, point at the **neutral face**, set the angle, press
Enter.

![Draft of 0°, 8° and 16°: the bottom face stays put while the walls open upwards.](img/part-draft/)

## What the neutral face is

It is the face that does not change: the tilt is measured from it. Usually it is the parting plane of
the mould — the way the part sits in the tooling.

## What it is for

A cast or moulded part will not come out of the mould without draft: the walls must open slightly
towards the parting line. Typical values are from half a degree to three, depending on the material
and the wall height.

## Order

Draft goes **before** the fillets: a fillet on a drafted face comes out right, draft on a filleted
one hardly will.
