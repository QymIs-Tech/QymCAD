# Split faces

Click a plane, a datum or a face, set the offset, press Enter. The command cuts a **surface**, not
material: there was one face, now there are two in the same plane. The body stays whole and nothing
falls off.

## What it is for

- Painting part of a surface a different colour or giving it a different finish.
- Setting a **machining region**: milling a piece of a plane rather than all of it.
- Getting a face for a draft or a shell where there were no edges.
- Giving a sketch or a joint an anchor exactly where it is needed.

## What cuts it

A plane, a datum or another face. The cut line stays tied to whatever cut it: move the datum and the
line follows.

## Do not confuse it with “split body”

[Split body](part/13-split-body) turns one body into two. Here there is one body and two faces: once
the part you wanted can be selected on its own, it is worked with like any other face.
