# Polygon

Key **G**. A regular polygon; the number of sides and the way it is fitted are set in the top bar.

![A regular hexagon: constraints keep it regular, and the circumscribed circle sets its size.](img/sketch-polygon.png)

The first click places the centre, the second a vertex or the middle of a side, depending on what is
chosen above.

## What you get

The sides are tied by equal length and by angles, so the polygon is defined by **a centre and one
dimension** — the radius of the circumscribed or of the inscribed circle.

## A hint

A hex for a spanner is easier to set by the **inscribed** circle: the across-flats size is exactly
the distance between opposite faces, that is twice the inscribed radius.
