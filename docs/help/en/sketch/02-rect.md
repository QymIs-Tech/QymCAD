# Rectangle

Key **R**. Three ways to define it, the switch is in the top bar.

![A rectangle by two corners; horizontals and verticals are placed for you.](img/sketch-rect.png)

- **By two corners** — click the opposite corners.
- **From the centre** — the first click sets the centre, the second a corner.
- **By three points** — two points set a side (and the tilt), the third sets the width. This is the
  only way to get a tilted rectangle without rotating it afterwards with constraints.

## What you get

Four segments with constraints: pairwise parallel and perpendicular, the corners match. That is why
a rectangle needs only **two dimensions** — width and height — to become fully defined.

## A hint

The centre rectangle is handy when the part is symmetric about the origin: the centre lands on zero
and symmetry comes for free, without a pair of dimensions.
